// what you can do with it

A commons you can train on. A commons you can teach.

OpenWALDO cuts both ways: pull the corpus and build models with a verifiable pedigree — or push your own knowledge into the substrate that models learn from.

for companies

Put your product into the model — on the record, on your terms.

Today, whether an AI model knows your product is an accident of web crawling. With OpenWALDO it’s a deliberate, first-class contribution: openly license your documentation, guides, specifications, and knowledge bases; ingest them with one command; and open a pull request. Every model trained on the tree afterward knows your product — and its bill of materials records exactly where that knowledge came from.

Think of it as the honest successor to SEO for the AI era: not scraping and guessing, but consented, licensed, attributed knowledge — contributed by the people who know it best.

Product knowledge

Ship your docs, tutorials, API references, and release notes into the commons so assistants answer questions about your product correctly — with provenance instead of hallucination.

Internal & domain models

The same toolchain works on a private tree: waldo index init, ingest your internal data, and train or fork domain models — support, engineering, legal — with a BOM your compliance team can actually read.

Auditable AI for regulated work

Finance, healthcare, government: when the auditor asks “what is this model built from?”, the answer is a signed, hash-pinned record — not a shrug. License partitioning and takedown are built into the format.

your knowledge → every model’s knowledge
# gather what you want models to know — docs, guides, KBs — openly licensed
$ waldo index ingest ./product-docs vendors/acme/docs \
      --name acme-docs --license CC-BY-4.0
manifest: vendors/acme/docs/acme-docs.json — tree verified ok
$ git commit -s -m "Add ACME product documentation" && git push
# open the PR — from now on, every model trained on the tree knows ACME
for builders & institutions

Models with a pedigree, wherever provenance matters.

Sovereign & public AI

Governments and public institutions get what no vendor can sell them: a model whose entire training input is inspectable, license-clean, and rebuildable inside their own borders. Where it was trained stops mattering, because what it was trained on is on the record.

Reproducible research

A published model that anyone can replay: same index commit, same data, same recipe, same architecture. Ablations and dataset studies become diffs against a pinned tree instead of archaeology.

Libraries & archives

Every institution that opens a public-domain collection under genuinely open terms is one fetch-and-ingest away from putting it into the training commons — with provenance and attribution preserved.

Education

The growth ladder starts on a laptop: students train a real model on real, licensed data in an afternoon and can inspect every byte of it. The whole loop — ingest, train, chat, export — in about ten minutes.

Forking & specialization

Fork a base model, continue training on your indexed data, and ship it — lineage preserved, both BOM chains intact. Specialization without losing the audit trail.

Clean-room model supply

Building a product on a model? Know exactly what you’re shipping. Per-shard SPDX tags let you build from precisely the license profile your product allows — include or exclude by tag.

// the incentive

The corpus is the commons. The models are proof it works.

Everything you contribute is carried — by hash, by license, by name — in every model that learns from it.
