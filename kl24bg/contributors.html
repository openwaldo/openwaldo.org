founding sponsor

Companies take part too.

CIQ

CIQ is the founding sponsor of OpenWALDO — an infrastructure software company built on open source, supporting the communities and technologies that run serious computing. Sponsorship here means what it says: backing the commons, not owning it. The index, the toolchain, and the models belong to the community, license-clean and forkable, no matter whose logo appears on this page.

ciq.com ↗

What sponsorship supports

Sponsors keep the unglamorous parts moving: engineering time on the toolchain, hosting and mirrors for shard storage, CI that re-verifies every data PR, and compute for the growth ladder’s bigger rungs. In keeping with the project’s funding-honest roadmap, what sponsorship buys is progress — never control.
