From a first homelab model to research and production infrastructure, WALDO is where people build, train, learn, contribute, and make the source of AI available to everyone.

02 / Participate in the corpus

Put useful knowledge
on the record.

The next generation of open AI should learn from material people can identify, inspect, and use under declared terms. Individuals and organizations can help expand that foundation.

FOR PEOPLE

Contribute or review a corpus.

Bring eligible public material, propose a reviewed acquisition recipe, improve source evidence, question a license assertion, or help audit what is already indexed.

How contribution works ↗Follow the walkthrough ↗
FOR ORGANIZATIONS

Make your public knowledge available to open AI.

Companies, projects, institutions, and communities can contribute redistributable public documentation with clear origin and license evidence—so future open models can include authoritative material rather than an unexplained scrape.
