02 / Grow the corpus

Contribute to the
public corpus.

Put useful knowledge on the record. Individuals and organizations can contribute material people can identify, inspect, and use under declared terms—or help review what is already there. Every submitted index change carries an attributable DCO sign-off.

FOR PEOPLE

Contribute or review corpora.

Bring eligible public material, propose a reviewed acquisition recipe, improve source evidence, question a license assertion, or help audit what is already indexed.

How contribution works ↗Review the workflow ↗
FOR ORGANIZATIONS

Make your public knowledge available to open source AI.

Companies, projects, institutions, and communities can contribute redistributable public documentation with clear origin and license evidence—so future open models can include authoritative material rather than an unexplained scrape.

03 / Build WALDO

Be part of the
WALDO project.

Open source needs more than code. WALDO needs support, documentation, testing, design, advocacy, leadership, reviewers, and maintainers. If you see a place where you can help, reach out to the project lead (Gregory M. Kurtzer) and start the conversation. For private security reports, email gmkurtzer@gmail.com or gmk@ciq.com instead of opening a public issue.
