contributing data

Two ways to acquire it. One path into the index.

Start with files you already have, or make acquisition reproducible with a strict ingest recipe and reviewed external fetchers. Both enter the same WALDO-owned conversion, audit, publication, and Git-review pipeline.

direct

Local files or directories

Give WALDO text, Markdown, JSONL, compressed JSONL, or raw Parquet and declare the corpus source, title, license assertion, and source category.

waldo index ingest ./acquired-data ./waldo-index/core/example
reproducible

Reviewed ingest recipe

A strict recipe names external fetcher commands and owns corpus metadata. WALDO hashes the recipe and executables, then runs them in private temporary input space.

waldo index ingest ./recipes/example.yaml ./waldo-index/core/example

what review sees

The pull request is small. The evidence is not.

Large training objects never enter Git. The review overlay carries the compact facts needed to understand, reproduce, and challenge the contribution.

S

Sources

Where the material came from, how it was acquired, and which upstream facts were observed.

L

License assertions

The declared license and supporting evidence stay attributable and reviewable; a hash never substitutes for legal judgment.

R

Recipe identity

Conversion, writer, tokenizer, fetcher, and ingest-recipe identities are pinned rather than implied.

H

Hashes and totals

Every canonical shard carries its object identity, location, document and token counts, license partition, and encoded size.

D

DCO sign-off

A named contributor attests that the contribution can be submitted under the declared terms; reviewers can inspect and challenge that assertion.

A

Independent audit

CI and reviewers can verify metadata, object availability, hashes, canonical records, duplicates, and manifest totals.

04

show your source

Make the claim visible—and link it to the record.

The badge is an invitation to inspect the model’s OpenWALDO provenance. Put it on a model card, README, or product page and link it to the BOM or provenance page that supports the claim.

A badge is a signpost, not proof by itself. The linked provenance is the substance.
